# Homepage keyword and visual update

The owner-supplied keyword list guides this revision. Search volume, difficulty and CPC figures have not been independently verified and are not published as claims.

| Keyword | Placement |
| --- | --- |
| AI Powered Digital Marketing Agency in Hyderabad | Homepage H1, SEO title and meta description |
| digital marketing agency in hyd | Natural Hyderabad wording in the hero and location FAQ |
| digital marketing service company in india | Small-business planning section |
| digital marketing services for business growth | Main services heading |
| best digital marketing services for small business growth in india | Natural variants in the small-business section and FAQ, framed as advice for choosing suitable services |

The copy explains SEO and AI search, paid advertising, website development, AI-assisted research with human review, budgets, delivery and enquiry measurement. It avoids ranking promises, invented results and an unsupported claim to be the best agency.

Three new responsive illustrations explain local discovery, audience/offer alignment and the website enquiry journey. The existing hero diagram is restyled in brand blue, giving four primary service visuals. Illustrations are built with HTML, CSS and vector icons, with accessible descriptions and real text captions. They do not depend on external image downloads or present sample metrics as client results.

The existing logo, Contact Us navigation, WhatsApp shortcut, native enquiry form, internal service links and goal selector are retained. Generated title, description and social metadata inherit the updated homepage metadata. Canonical and indexing configuration are unchanged.

The colour, FAQ and CTA refinement uses a navy hero, blue primary buttons, pale-blue support sections and coordinated card borders. Eight concise FAQs cover enquiries, service choice, AI use, coverage, budget, timing, existing assets and lead quality. They use native keyboard-operable disclosures in separate white cards. The goal selector now links to Contact with the relevant service preselected. The hero, service and FAQ enquiry actions remain available. The separate closing CTA after the FAQs was removed at the owner?s request. Styles are scoped to `.home-page` in `src/styles/home-refinements.css`.

Historical validation for the visual revision: type checking, production build, three generated-page tests and 95 browser route/width checks passed. See [verification history](QA.md) for checks after the later section removal. Mobile and desktop screenshots include each new illustration. Form checks use mocked requests.

Editorial reference: [Google Search Central: helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
