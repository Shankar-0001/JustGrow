# JustGrow content review — 26 October 2026

This review records the audit snapshot. Afterwards, the owner requested removal of all sections after FAQs, including related-service cards and duplicate closing CTAs. All 16 pages with FAQs now end at that section; the shared footer and earlier service navigation remain. Website-development links before the FAQs remain in place.

Reviewed the source and rendered content for all 19 public routes. Repeated layout headings are part of the shared template; the service scope, process, FAQs and measurement copy are specific to each service. Existing navy/blue styling, the original logo, native Google Sheets form and WhatsApp link were explicitly retained by the owner.

Every service has a relevant enquiry route, useful FAQs and contextual service links. Website-development measurement and related links were restored in this audit. No new URLs, articles, client projects, testimonials or statistics were invented. A heading-order scan found no skipped heading levels in the main content of the 19 pages.

The following references informed the review; they are audit references, not added promotional copy on the website:

- Google says its AI search features build on established SEO practices and do not require special AI files or schema. This supports the restrained wording in the AEO/GEO FAQs. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).
- OpenAI describes advertising as labelled and separate from answers. The site's paid-versus-organic distinction is consistent with that description; an individual advertiser's eligibility remains unverified. [OpenAI advertising information](https://ads.openai.com/).
- Structured data remains limited to visible business facts, website, breadcrumbs and service descriptions. No ratings, reviews, fake results or visibility guarantees were added.

Site content is not proof of business results, platform eligibility or Google indexing. Those checks require the relevant accounts and operational evidence.
